Technical SEO Audit for Websites That Are Not Reaching Their Potential

Search engines have to crawl, render and understand your pages before they can rank them. A technical audit looks for the barriers that get in the way, then turns them into a short, ordered list of fixes.

Problems this solves

  • Important pages are not indexed, or the wrong pages are.
  • Duplicate or near-duplicate URLs split signals between pages.
  • Mobile pages load slowly or shift while loading.
  • Broken links and long redirect chains waste crawl effort.
  • Canonical tags, titles or structured data are missing or contradictory.

What you receive

  • Crawl and indexation review, using Search Console data if you give me access
  • Site structure and internal linking review
  • Mobile usability and page speed check with free public tools
  • Review of robots.txt, XML sitemap, redirects and canonical tags
  • Structured data check for errors and missing opportunities
  • Prioritised action list (high, medium, low) written in plain English

How it works

  1. 1. Access and goals

    You share the website and, ideally, read-only Search Console access, plus what the site needs to achieve.

  2. 2. Crawl and review

    I crawl the site and check indexing, speed, mobile and markup issues page by page.

  3. 3. Prioritise

    Findings are ranked by likely impact and effort so the first fixes are the most useful ones.

  4. 4. Walkthrough

    You receive the report and a call or written summary so your developer knows exactly what to change.

Benefits

  • A clear picture of what may be holding the site back
  • Fixes ranked so effort goes where it matters
  • Notes your developer or CMS admin can follow without guessing

Not included

  • Making the fixes in your CMS (can be scoped separately)
  • Link building
  • Any guarantee of ranking changes

Technical SEO Audit FAQ

Do I need Search Console set up?
It helps a lot because it shows what Google actually indexed. If you do not have it, I can explain how to add it and run a lighter review with public tools in the meantime.
Will you fix the problems?
The audit identifies and prioritises issues. Fixes can be done by your developer from my notes, or scoped as separate work if I can do them on your platform.
How is this different from an automated report?
Tools find symptoms. The audit explains which ones matter for your site and in what order to deal with them.

Best suited to

Businesses whose pages are missing from Google, sites that have just been redesigned or migrated, and owners who have never had their site reviewed technically.
