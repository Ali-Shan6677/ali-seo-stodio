Keyword Research Built Around What Your Customers Actually Search For

Good keyword research is not a long list of terms. It shows which searches matter to your business, what the searcher wants, and which page on your site should answer them.

Problems this solves

  • Pages target terms nobody searches, or terms that do not bring customers.
  • Content is written without checking the search intent behind a term.
  • UK and US audiences use different words for the same service.
  • There is no clear plan for which page should target which topic.

What you receive

  • Keyword map in a spreadsheet: term, intent, page, priority
  • Search intent notes: informational, commercial or local
  • Topic groups so related searches go on the same page
  • Quick-win opportunities where the site already has some visibility
  • UK and US wording comparison where relevant
  • Notes on terms to avoid because they attract the wrong visitors

How it works

  1. 1. Understand the business

    We define services, margins, locations and the customers you most want.

  2. 2. Collect and group

    I gather candidate searches from tools and search results, then group them by topic.

  3. 3. Check intent

    For each topic I look at what currently ranks to see what searchers expect.

  4. 4. Map and prioritise

    Topics are assigned to existing or new pages and ranked by value and effort.

Benefits

  • One page, one clear topic
  • Less guesswork about what to publish next
  • Wording that suits UK or US customers

Not included

  • Search volume guarantees: tool estimates are only estimates
  • Writing the pages themselves
  • Paid search keyword management

Keyword Research FAQ

Which tools do you use?
I use Google Search Console data where available plus keyword and SERP tools. [LIST THE TOOLS YOU ACTUALLY USE]
Are search volumes accurate?
They are estimates and vary between tools. I use them to compare topics against each other, not as promises.
Do I need this if I already have a site?
Yes, if pages overlap or you are unsure what each one should target. The map can also guide what to add next.

Best suited to

Businesses planning a new site or new pages, owners unsure what to write about, and sites targeting both UK and US customers.
