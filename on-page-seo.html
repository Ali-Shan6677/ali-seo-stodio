On-Page SEO That Makes Every Important Page Easier to Find and Understand

Many pages fail because they do not clearly say what they are about, or they target a search that the page does not actually answer. On-page SEO fixes that one page at a time.

Problems this solves

  • Several pages compete for the same search term.
  • Title tags and meta descriptions are generic or cut off in results.
  • Headings do not follow a logical outline.
  • Service or category pages have too little helpful detail.
  • Pages barely link to each other.

What you receive

  • Target search term and intent for each priority page
  • Rewritten title tags and meta descriptions
  • Heading outline for each page
  • Content gap notes: what the page should explain but does not
  • Internal link suggestions with suggested anchor text
  • Image alt text and basic markup recommendations
  • A tracking sheet you can tick off as changes go live

How it works

  1. 1. Choose priority pages

    We agree which pages matter most to the business, usually service, location or category pages.

  2. 2. Match page to search intent

    Each page is paired with the search it should answer, based on keyword research.

  3. 3. Write recommendations

    You get page-by-page copy and structure changes, not a list of generic tips.

  4. 4. Review after changes

    Once changes are live, I check them and note anything to adjust.

Benefits

  • Each page has a clear job and a clear target
  • Better-looking search snippets that explain the page
  • Fewer pages competing against each other

Not included

  • Writing full new long-form pages (see content strategy)
  • Redesigning your website
  • Guaranteed ranking or traffic increases

On-Page SEO FAQ

How many pages can you cover?
It depends on the package and page type. A focused set of 5 to 10 key pages is a sensible start.
Will you edit my website directly?
By default I deliver recommendations and copy. Direct edits are possible on some platforms if we agree it in advance.
Is on-page SEO a one-time job?
Pages should be reviewed when your services or search behaviour change, but the first round usually creates the biggest cleanup.

Best suited to

Businesses with a decent website that has vague titles, thin service pages or product categories with little useful copy.
