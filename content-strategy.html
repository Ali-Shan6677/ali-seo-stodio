SEO Content Strategy That Turns Expertise Into Pages People Search For

Content works best for SEO when it answers real questions, is organised around clear topics, and links to the pages that turn readers into enquiries. This service builds that plan and the briefs to execute it.

Problems this solves

  • Blog posts are published without a plan or a topic focus.
  • Writers do not know what each article must cover.
  • Older pages are outdated and never reviewed.
  • Content does not link to service pages or lead to an enquiry.

What you receive

  • Topic cluster map: core pages and supporting articles
  • Detailed content briefs: intent, outline, questions to answer, internal links
  • Simple publishing calendar matched to your capacity
  • Refresh list for existing content
  • Guidance on adding genuine experience and examples from your business
  • Calls to action planned for each article

How it works

  1. 1. Pick the topics

    Using keyword and competitor research, we choose the topics that suit your services.

  2. 2. Build the cluster map

    I show how pages relate so they can support each other through internal links.

  3. 3. Write the briefs

    Each brief tells the writer what to cover, in what order and what to link to.

  4. 4. Plan and review

    We agree a realistic schedule and check performance after publication.

Benefits

  • A plan that fits your resources
  • Writers get clear instructions
  • Articles connected to services and enquiries

Not included

  • Publishing mass AI-generated content
  • Guaranteed rankings for any article
  • Social media management

SEO Content Strategy FAQ

Do you write the articles?
The core service is strategy and briefs. Writing can be scoped separately, or your own team or writer can use the briefs.
How many briefs are included?
It depends on the package. A batch of 5 to 10 briefs is a common starting point.
How do you make content original?
Briefs ask for real examples, photos and expertise from your business, which generic content cannot offer.

Best suited to

Businesses that want to publish helpful content but are unsure what to write, and teams with a writer who needs clear direction.
