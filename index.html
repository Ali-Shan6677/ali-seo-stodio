Industries I focus on

Different businesses are found in different ways. These are the opportunities I usually look for. Opportunities are not guarantees, and results depend on competition and the work done.

Home service businesses

People search when something breaks or needs doing. Clear service pages, local profiles and fast mobile pages help you appear when the need is urgent.

Plumbing and contracting companies

Separate pages for each service and area, consistent business details and a steady review process can improve how you appear in local results.

Car rental businesses

Pages for each location, vehicle type and rental length, plus the right local wording (for example "car hire" in the UK), can capture more specific searches.

Local service providers

Clinics, salons, tutors and similar businesses benefit from accurate listings, helpful FAQs and pages built around the questions customers ask.

Small business websites

Small sites often need the basics done well: clear titles, a logical structure, useful pages and working tracking.

E-commerce businesses

Category pages, product descriptions, duplicate URLs from filters and internal linking are the usual starting points.

Frequently asked questions

How long does SEO take to show results?
It depends on competition, the site's current state and how quickly changes are made. Some improvements can appear within weeks, but meaningful growth in organic traffic and leads commonly takes a few months. I will not promise a fixed timeline.
What is included in an SEO audit?
A review of crawling and indexing, page titles and content, mobile usability and speed, internal linking, local signals where relevant, and a prioritised list of recommendations. The free report is a lighter, manual version, described on the contact page.
Do you work with UK and US businesses?
Yes. I am based in Pakistan and work remotely with clients in the UK, the US and other English-speaking markets. I adapt spelling, search wording and directories to the market.
Can you work with an existing website?
Yes. Most projects start with an existing site. I work with common platforms such as WordPress, Shopify, Wix and custom sites, and make recommendations your developer can follow.
Do you provide monthly SEO services?
Yes, through the Ongoing SEO Support package after a foundation is in place. Monthly work is usually most useful once the main issues have been fixed.
How do you report SEO progress?
With a plain-English report using data from Search Console and analytics: what was done, what changed, what it means for leads and what comes next.
Do you guarantee first-page Google rankings?
No. Nobody outside Google can guarantee rankings, and anyone who does should be treated with caution. I commit to a clear process, honest reporting and ethical methods.
