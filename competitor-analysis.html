Competitor SEO Analysis: See What Is Working for the Sites You Compete With

The businesses you compete with for customers are not always the ones you compete with in search results. This analysis identifies the real competitors and shows where your site can do better.

Problems this solves

  • You do not know which sites actually take your search traffic.
  • Competitors cover topics and pages that you have not published.
  • It is unclear how competitor pages are structured or why they appear.
  • You cannot tell which gaps are worth closing first.

What you receive

  • List of true search competitors for your main topics
  • Content gap table: topics they cover that you do not
  • Page structure notes for the pages that rank best
  • High-level link profile overview using available tools
  • SERP feature notes: maps, FAQs, shopping, videos
  • A short opportunities list, ranked by effort and likely value

How it works

  1. 1. Pick the set

    We choose the competitors who appear for your most valuable searches.

  2. 2. Compare coverage

    I compare topics, pages and structure against your site.

  3. 3. Spot patterns

    I look for what the best-ranking pages have in common and where they are weak.

  4. 4. Recommend

    You receive a ranked list of opportunities, not a data dump.

Benefits

  • A realistic view of the search landscape
  • Clear, ranked opportunities
  • Less time copying competitors blindly

Not included

  • Copying competitor content
  • Spying on private data
  • Guaranteeing you will outrank any named competitor

Competitor SEO Analysis FAQ

Do I need to name my competitors?
It helps, but I also find the ones that show up in search for your key topics.
Will you tell me their exact traffic?
No tool gives exact figures for other sites. I use estimates for comparison and say so clearly.
Is this useful for a new website?
Yes. It shows what a new site must cover to compete and where to begin.

Best suited to

Businesses in crowded markets, owners who feel competitors always appear above them, and anyone planning an SEO strategy.
